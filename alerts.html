Google Calendar can take hours to fetch a new subscription, and ignores alarms from subscriptions (set notifications on that calendar yourself). For an instant start on Android, use the free ICSx⁵ app: paste the link, subscribe, and the events appear in Google Calendar within a minute.

To turn it off: remove the calendar in Google Calendar (Settings → your account → the Phx calendar → Unsubscribe) or delete it in ICSx⁵.

💬 WhatsApp me (raid alert buttons)

A one-tap button other members can press to message you on WhatsApp, such as "🚨 Raided". Your number is never shown, and each button can be tapped once an hour per person.
  1. In your browser, open create.wa.link, enter your number and a message (for example "🚨 Raided"), and copy the short link it gives you (like wa.link/abc123).
  2. Open My Account → WhatsApp Alert Buttons.
  3. Type a label, paste your link, and tap + Add Button.
  4. Check it appears on the Raid Alert page.
To turn it off: delete the button in My Account → WhatsApp Alert Buttons.
