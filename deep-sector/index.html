How to play
  • Each commander hides four ships in an 8×8 sector: Bastion (4), Lancer (3), Corsair (3) and Wisp (2).
  • Tap your sector to place the highlighted ship. Rotate turns it (or press R), tapping a placed ship picks it back up.
  • Take turns firing at the enemy sector. A hit earns you another shot; a miss passes the turn.
  • Once per round you can use a Sensor sweep instead of firing: pick a cell and it tells you how many ship sections sit in that 3×3 area.
  • Destroy every enemy ship to win the round. Wins carry over, so keep a running score.
  • Every move is saved, so you don't both need to be online. Take your shot whenever you open the link.
